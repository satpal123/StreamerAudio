Streamer Audio Support

Streamer Audio is an independent app and is not affiliated with Twitch.

Before reporting a problem

  • Confirm your device is online.
  • Try Direct routing in Settings.
  • If you configured a proxy, verify its HTTPS URL and operator status.
  • For sign-in problems, log out and sign in again.
  • For Android Auto, confirm Streamer Audio is enabled as a media app and test playback on the phone first.

Get help

Open an issue in the Streamer Audio issue tracker. Include the app version, Android version, device model, routing mode, and reproducible steps. Never post an OAuth token, proxy credential, signing key or other secret.
