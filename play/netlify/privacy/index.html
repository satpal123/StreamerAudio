Streamer Audio Privacy Policy

Effective date: Replace before publication

Streamer Audio is an independent audio player for public Twitch live streams. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Twitch Interactive, Inc.

Information and services

Twitch sign-in is optional. If you sign in, the app receives a Twitch access token plus your Twitch account identifier and login name. It uses these to validate your session and request followed-channel and public catalog information from Twitch. The token and account details are encrypted on your device using Android Keystore and kept in storage excluded from backup.

Guest browsing sends public catalog requests to the Streamer Audio backend hosted on Netlify. Twitch, Netlify and ordinary internet infrastructure may receive network metadata such as your IP address and user agent under their own policies.

Direct playback is the default. When you are signed in, the app may send your OAuth token directly to Twitch while requesting playback so Twitch can apply account entitlements. If Twitch does not accept authenticated playback, the app retries without the account token. OAuth tokens are never sent to a custom proxy. If you add and select a custom proxy, the app sends the selected channel name and audio/source request options to that endpoint. The proxy operator can receive the request and normal network metadata. Streamer Audio does not supply or operate those endpoints; review an operator's policy before using one.

On-device storage and retention

The app stores non-sensitive preferences such as favorite channels, recently played channels, proxy names and HTTPS URLs, routing choice and sleep-timer state in Android DataStore. Favorites and recent history remain only on your device and are available in guest and signed-in modes. You can remove favorites, clear recent history, and edit or delete proxies in the app. Authentication data remains until logout, token invalidation, app-data deletion or storage corruption. Logout attempts to revoke the token with Twitch, deletes local authentication, stops playback, and retains non-sensitive preferences and the local library.

Permissions and sharing

Internet and network-state access support catalog and playback requests. Notification, wake-lock and media-playback foreground-service permissions support background audio and Android Auto controls. Version 2.0 contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDK.

Your choices

You can use guest browsing, avoid custom proxies, log out at any time, delete individual proxy entries, or clear/uninstall the app to remove its local data.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Streamer Audio support page. This policy may be updated when app behavior or legal requirements change.
